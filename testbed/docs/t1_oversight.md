# Human oversight and escalation procedure

## Human review
Support agents perform human review of a daily sample of conversations and of every conversation flagged by a customer.

## Escalation
Customers can ask to be transferred to a human at any time. The assistant must hand off to a human for refund disputes, complaints and any request it cannot answer from the passages.

## Override and stop button
Support leads can override any answer and use the stop button in the admin console to disable the assistant immediately.
