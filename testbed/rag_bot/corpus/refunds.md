# Refunds

Refunds are processed within 14 days of approval.

To request a refund, open the customer portal, choose the invoice, and select Request refund. Duplicate charges are refunded in full once verified.

Refunds are paid back to the original payment method. If that method is no longer valid, support will ask for alternative details through the portal.

Goodwill credits after a service outage are decided case by case by the support team.
