# Late fees

A late fee of 5 EUR is charged if a bill remains unpaid 7 days after the due date.

The late fee is charged once per invoice and does not increase over time.

If you contact support before the due date, you can ask to defer payment by up to 2 weeks. A deferral is not a guarantee and is decided by the support team.

Late fees may be waived once per customer after a first late payment.
