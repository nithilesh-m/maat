# Billing cycle

ExampleShop bills customers once a month. Your bill is due on the 5th of each month.

Your billing period runs from the 1st to the last day of the previous month. The invoice is generated on the 1st and sent by email.

If the 5th falls on a weekend or a public holiday, payment is still due on the 5th, but payments received on the next working day are not treated as late.

You can see the amount due and the due date at any time in the customer portal under Billing.
